Real experience on the ground

We organise sports events such as LARRE Long Distance, a 214 km non-stop, self-supported ultra-trail through the mountains of Álava, run by Musubigara®, the team behind Mugarri.

Over 30 years of track record

A long track record organising sports and cultural events, with established contacts in the gravel cycling and trail running sector.

Accommodation and environment

We have active agreements with more than 50 accommodations, ready to host participants and events. Projects are designed to leave minimal impact on the environment, with a particular interest in km0 suppliers.
