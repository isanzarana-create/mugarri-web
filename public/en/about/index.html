Who we are

Mugarri is an artisan active-tourism project born from Musubigara®, a Basque cultural and sports management brand. Together with a network of allied companies, we design gravel and trail running experiences with the same care and attention to detail, whether crafting a route, producing an event or supporting clients on the ground.

We know the sector from both sides: we ride it — gravel bikes and trail shoes — and we manage it professionally, with over 30 years of combined experience. That dual perspective lets us understand what every accommodation, supplier, and rider actually needs. Each collaboration is custom, tailored, mimo — because no product is the same.

Among our projects, we organise endurance events such as LARRE Long Distance — a 214 km self-supported ultra-trail through the mountains of Álava — giving our partners the real-world confidence of a team that knows how to run high-level events on the ground.
